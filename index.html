The five parts of a video

The claim What you are arguing. Written first, never said out loud.
The hook The first three seconds. A strange thing to look at, and a line that does not follow from it.
What it is Teach something real and checkable. Twenty to thirty seconds.
So what Your story, and the bit underneath you would rather not say. Your words only — nobody writes this one for you.
Over to you One small thing tonight, the dispute line, then the sign-off.

The claim test

Could a sensible person argue with this out loud?

If nobody could, it is not a claim. It is a fact or a feeling, and the video will have no spine. Put it back in the vault.

If the week falls apart

One video a week is a complete week.

There are no streaks. Nothing accumulates. You do not owe next week anything.

The floor: one Strange Things I Learnt. No props, no set, no shoot day, nobody else. You and a table, ninety seconds.

And: make that one video before you are allowed to improve the system. Rebuilding is avoidance that feels like work.

What you shoot Wednesday goes out next week. You are always a week ahead, so nothing is ever due today.
